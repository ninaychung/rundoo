Rundoo Flooring GTM Plan

Rundoo enters flooring in two phases: refine the product and run a trial now, then launch officially in 2027 with broad reach, trusted partnerships and targeted direct sales.

Summary and ideal customer

Two phases, one customer profile

Now to December 2026

Phase 1

Refine the product for flooring and run a trial with 5 stores.

January to June 2027

Phase 2

Official launch with three goals: broad awareness, trusted partnerships, and targeted direct sales.

Ideal customer profile. Independent flooring store owners running on paper, spreadsheets, or QuickBooks alone, with a recent change such as a new store location, a second-generation takeover, or a change of ownership. At these moments owners are actively looking for new solutions and are willing to try new software.

Where to start. Stores that hold inventory, because they best fit Rundoo's existing product, like counter sales and inventory management. Once the flooring-specific features ship, expand to stores that sell mostly by the job with little or no inventory.

Phase 1: now to December 2026

Three priorities, in order

1. Product refinements for flooring

Build in this order:

  • Main: SKUs and inventory management specific to flooring: color, flooring type, and square feet.
  • Secondary: ringing up a customer includes estimate and installation costs, plus estimate and install scheduling (see the product feature).
  • Secondary: pull and track stock directly from manufacturers. This needs supplier integrations, which can take a long time. Start with a link to the manufacturer's site stamped with when it was last checked, then move to fcB2B, the flooring industry's standard for exchanging catalog, price and stock data.
  • Third: auto-assign an estimator and an installer.
  • Later: connecting a store's existing website or a room visualizer to Rundoo. It risks distracting from the core product, so revisit it once pilots are live.

2. Trial program with 5 stores (main priority)

Offer a low price for the first year (ex: $600). In exchange, the store works closely with us on bugs and improvements and agrees to be a named case study. Pick pilots from the ideal customer profile, mostly stores that hold inventory, and include one or two job-driven stores to learn what the next wave needs.

3. Partner groundwork (second priority)

Build a list of manufacturers, associations and buying groups, introduce Rundoo, and see who is open to a partnership or to promoting us. Shortlist the conferences worth attending, confirm their dates, and start planning.

Phase 2: January to June 2027

Official launch, three goals

Goal 1: Broad reach and visibility (main priority, starting January)

Online presence. Rebuild Rundoo's pages for flooring, publish customer stories from the pilot stores, and write flooring blog content. The aim is to beat Service Buddy and Floorzap in search. Our best prospects are high intent: a new owner or a new store is likely typing "best system for a flooring store" into Google or an AI assistant, and whoever shows up then gets the demo.

Events. Owners may be skeptical and want hands-on proof, and a booth or live demo gives them that. Exhibit or attend TISE (Feb 2027) and Coverings (April 2027), sponsor smaller regional or design events, and pitch pilot owners as speakers and interview subjects for flooring trade magazines.

Who's responsibleThe product manager defines the flooring story and content plan, and the marketing team executes it.

Goal 2: Partnerships for distribution and trust

Look to work with fcB2B, flooring manufacturers, buying groups, accreditation groups, and trade magazines. Focus on manufacturer reps, who are the trusted source in these stores.

Collaborations could look like:

  • A member offer, for example 20% off the first year for a buying group's members.
  • A customer story or guest article in the group's newsletter or magazine.
  • A demo slot or listing at the group's meetings.

Who's responsibleThe product manager.

Goal 3: Direct sales

  1. Start with existing referrals. Rundoo's current clients and the pilot stores are the warmest leads.
  2. Source new stores with strong ideal-customer fit. Scan local town news and public business listings for new flooring businesses, grand openings and ownership changes. Those are our triggers.
  3. Test outreach. Try email, physical mail and phone calls to sourced stores, and measure replies before scaling. The sequence is cold call, then demo, then an integration call.
  4. Walk in. Sales reps visit the best-fit stores in person with a brochure or short slide deck built from existing Rundoo materials, with flooring client stories and numbers swapped in.

Who's responsibleThe product manager builds the sales deck and talk track, and the sales team runs the outreach and visits.

Targets, unit economics and budget

Cost to reach 100 stores by June 2027, including the pilots

At an assumed $3,600 a year per store, 100 stores is about $360,000 of annual revenue. If 10% to 20% of stores come through referrals, trade shows and newsletters and the rest are field-sold, the plan costs much less per customer than a store pays in its first year.
